What is a Magic Square?

The Mathematical Beauty

A magic square is a square grid of numbers where the sums of all rows, columns, and both main diagonals are exactly the same. Ramanujan created a special 4×4 magic square where the first row shows his own birthday.

In Ramanujan's square the magic sum = Day + Month + Year-century + Year-decade holds true across all 4 rows, 4 columns, 2 diagonals, and many other symmetric sub-groups — a testament to the infinite elegance of numbers.
